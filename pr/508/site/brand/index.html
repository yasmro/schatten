Ink runs cool, paper runs warm — at identical chroma

sumi and alabaster share the same lightness and the same chroma; only the hue differs, 250 against 70. Both read as near-neutral, yet ink tilts blue and paper tilts yellow.

Seasons

Eight seasons, one lightness

A season (a Special theme) swaps only the --color-theme-* ramp. Every 500 rung sits at L 64%, every 700 rung at L 46% — hue and chroma move, nothing else. The dates match the classical eight markers: the four beginnings and the two solstices and equinoxes.

  • Spring begins2/4h12
  • Spring equinox3/21h138
  • Summer begins5/6h162
  • Summer solstice6/21h45
  • Autumn begins8/7h230
  • Autumn equinox9/23h70
  • Winter begins11/7h250
  • Winter solstice12/22h255

Seasons turn; legibility does not. The eight markers carry color alone and never touch the lightness ladder.

Type

Speaking two languages at once

The structure is Western engineering vocabulary — shadcn, Radix, OKLCH, BEM, CVA. The surface is Japanese aesthetic vocabulary — vermillion, ink, washi, indigo, the eight markers. Neither decorates the other.

That duality is not confined to names and colors; it is implemented in the typesetting. Both the sans and the serif role pair a Latin face with a Japanese face into a single stack. Being bilingual is the default state.
